## Objective

- **Do now:** Choose one IaC path, generate templates, validate them, and attempt deployment.
- **Input:** Approved requirements, architecture and cost evidence, SKU manifest, and current review findings.
- **Output:** Governance constraints, approved IaC plan and contracts, one IaC track, validation evidence, deployment summary, and workflow diagram.
- **Required to move on:** Governance reconciliation, approved plan review, one validated IaC path, explicit deployment authorization, a recorded deployment attempt, and the workflow explanation.
- **Decisions now:** Bicep vs Terraform, module boundaries, governance handling, and what evidence to keep.
- **Next:** C4 branches from your actual deployment outcome and extends the same platform for HA/DR.

This is the most execution-sensitive step in the sequence. Keep the work moving by
choosing one path, validating often, and recording what actually happened.

## The Business Challenge

Nordic Fresh Foods now needs production-ready infrastructure code that can be deployed
consistently, respects governance and security controls, and stays maintainable for a
small DevOps function. A design that looks good on paper but cannot be validated or
deployed is not enough.

## Your Tasks

1. **Discover governance (~5 min).** Select `04g-Governance` to capture effective policy constraints in `04-governance-constraints.md` and `.json`. If constraints exist, complete the required reconciliation review and approval.
2. **Plan the implementation (~8 min).** Select `05-IaC Planner` to create `04-implementation-plan.md` and the machine-readable contracts. Select `10-Challenger`, resolve blocking plan findings, and approve the plan.
3. **Generate one IaC track (~10 min).** Select `06b-Bicep CodeGen` or `06t-Terraform CodeGen` according to the approved requirements. Keep the approved plan read-only and retain `05-iac-handoff.json`.
4. **Validate and preview (~7 min).** Run the deterministic checks and Bicep what-if or Terraform plan. Validation success does not authorize deployment.
5. **Authorize and deploy (~10 min).** Review the proposed scope and current evidence as a team. Explicitly authorize the matching Deploy agent, then capture the result in `06-deployment-summary.md`. If deployment fails, preserve the exact blocker.
6. **Explain the compressed workshop path (~5 min).** Create `03-des-deployment-workflow.md` showing human handoffs, reviews, approval gates, validation, deployment, and return paths.

## Key Decisions

- Which IaC language gives your team the best chance of producing maintainable,
  reviewable code inside the workshop timebox?
- Which resources or concerns deserve separate modules, and which can stay simple in
  the first version?
- Which governance and security requirements must be built into the templates before
  you even try deployment?
- What evidence best proves that the templates are real, validated, and understood by
  the team?

## Deliverables

- `agent-output/freshconnect/04-governance-constraints.md` and `.json`
- Governance reconciliation evidence when effective constraints exist.
- `agent-output/freshconnect/04-implementation-plan.md` and its machine-readable contracts.
- Current plan review evidence with no unresolved blocking findings.
- IaC folder at `infra/bicep/freshconnect/` or `infra/terraform/freshconnect/`.
- `agent-output/freshconnect/05-iac-handoff.json`.
- Validation and preview evidence for the selected track.
- `agent-output/freshconnect/06-deployment-summary.md` and policy-precheck evidence.
- `agent-output/freshconnect/03-des-deployment-workflow.md` showing the real human-approved path.

## Success Criteria

| Focus | What good looks like | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance closure | Effective policy constraints are known before planning | Governance artifacts and reconciliation evidence are present or the no-constraints condition is recorded |
| Approved implementation strategy | The plan connects approved architecture, cost, SKU, and governance evidence to code generation | Plan contracts and current Challenger evidence are complete |
| Maintainable IaC | One selected track is structured for readability and reuse | The IaC folder follows the approved plan and includes the handoff artifact |
| Validation and authorization | The team separates deterministic validation from permission to deploy | Preview evidence exists and deployment authorization is explicit |
| Delivery understanding | The team can explain what happened and why | Deployment evidence and workflow diagram show handoffs, gates, failures, and return paths |

## Watch Out

- Governance discovery is not optional just because the challenge is time-boxed.
- A missing or failed Azure policy query does not prove that no constraints exist.
- `05-IaC Planner` owns plan corrections. CodeGen must not silently rewrite the approved plan.
- Validation success is not deployment success and does not grant deployment permission.
- Preserve the exact preview, policy, deployment, and application-health limits in the summary.
- Do not skip artifact names; later challenges and facilitator review depend on them.
